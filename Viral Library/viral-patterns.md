## Frame-by-frame analysis of each clip (2026-10-22)
Analysis folders (frames, timeline, contact sheet.jpg): `C:\dev\reels\3-analysed\<id>\` (same folder as the old Instagram reels). Replay curve = YouTube "most replayed", 100 bins, averaged per tenth of the clip.

### 1. tfiLeyu02oo, "Smartest Jynxzi Moment", 10 s, 44.2M views, owner's channel
- Layout: vertical split. Top: his face huge (zoomed cam). Bottom: Roblox Red-Light-Green-Light. Static caption "green light" + 😭 for the whole clip.
- Hook: zero setup. Second 0 he is already shouting "Green light!". Title is the promise ("Smartest... moment", skull emoji): you stay to see the trick.
- Beat: 2 cuts only (2.5 s, 6.7 s). Screen flashes red/green with game state. Peak at second 8: wide scream with green flash. Last words "Got him! It actually worked!" = payoff.
- Replay: 0.79 -> 0.07 (front-loaded; everyone rewatches the start, the tail is weak).

### 2. 1C75EYP-NsU, "Faker has his own tower in Korea", 20 s, 9.5M views
- Not a gameplay moment: a trivia explainer in his voice. Zoomed face over Rocket League footage; first 6 s a Faker image on the lower half, then full-frame face.
- Hook = a surprising claim as the first sentence: "Faker is so famous in Korea, more famous than some K-pop stars". Ends on a credibility punch: "I'm not even shitting you".
- Captions: ONE big word at a time, white with black outline, centre of the face, 1 word per beat (FAKER / FAMOUS / KOREA...). Picture inserts on the key noun (Faker Tower photo appears when he says "tower").
- All 20 s voiced, no dead air. 2 cuts. Matches the user's note: cropped-in, other people only sometimes, flashy.
- Replay: 0.74 -> 0.02 (steady fall: claims are front-loaded).

### 3. saPPnSCoRxM, "Ranking Top 5 clips that made Jynxzi Famous", 63 s, 4.7M views

- Entries (each ~10 s, loud rant/scream): 5 "Light work no reaction" (eating wings), 4 "Not James Bond" (shotgun vs pistol rant), 3 "That mf is cheating" (swears on the Bible), 2 "Bottlecap clipfarm", 1 "PTJBIIMA" (the Alpha-pack opening scream, shown with its 1M views thumbnail).

## What I can say, and what I cannot
Supported by this data (still n=6):
- The two biggest are the shortest (10 s, 20 s) and are single-idea, zoomed-face, no-dead-air, front-loaded hook, persistent or word-by-word caption.
- The same "Top 5" list format gave 4.7M, 93k and 32k, so the format alone does not explain views. Visible differences in the weak ones: longer (100 s), slowed, bigger black margins or smaller video, opaque/in-joke labels, long speechless stretches.
- Every replay curve falls steeply after the first tenth except #3 (mid peak): the first seconds decide everything.

NOT separable with this data: channel size, upload date (Aug 2026 vs Dec 2025 vs Mar 2025), thumbnails/titles, and YouTube's own push. Do not read these as causes. Need 20-50 clips including flops from the same channels to test them.

## Hypotheses to test (not conclusions)
H1 clips under ~25 s beat 30/45/60 s in this niche. H2 the first sentence is a claim or a conflict, not a setup. H3 word-by-word centre caption beats static caption. H4 a payoff in the last 3 s plus a pinned title promise. H5 compilations only win with a strong #1 and readable labels.

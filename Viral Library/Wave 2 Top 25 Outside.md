# Wave 2: top 25 outside clips about Jynxzi (read frame by frame, 2026-10-22)

Evidence: [[Wave 2 working table]] (rows 1-26, minus row 14). Pool = third-party Shorts and clips with Jynxzi in the title, ranked by views among the 28 analysed so far. The cycle queue is still running, so this is the first 25 to finish, not the whole internet. Audio numbers come from `study/data/<id>/features.json`. Labels: FACT (counted), SEEN (by eye), INFERENCE.

## 1. Basics (FACT)
- Views 25.9M to 1.26M (13th = 2.4M). The outside top 25 sits well below Wave 1 (own channel, top clip 44.2M).
- Length: median 22 s (range 12 to 75); Wave 1 median about 34 s. Outside clips are shorter.
- Cuts: median 7; only 7 of 25 have 5 or fewer cuts (Wave 1: 15 of 25). Outside editors cut more.
- Row 14 (Clash Royale animation, 16:9, 1.08M) is rank 28 and kept only as a contrast.

## 2. Layout (SEEN, counted)
- 18 of 25: vertical split or stack, Jynxzi (or a guest) on top about 40-55%, supporting media below (a friend's cam, game footage, a trailer, a still of the person discussed).
- 6 of 25: full-frame vertical (bed skit, esports-lounge vlog, selfie rant, bed confession, talk-show crop, the opening "hacked" selfie).
- 1 of 25: landscape gameplay with a facecam corner (the "bad aim" recreation).
- INFERENCE: the face-top, media-bottom split is the default recipe for outside editors; it is the same zone design as Wave 1, so the layout is shared across inside and outside.

## 3. Families (SEEN)
1. **Call or guest banter (two faces):** Dantes, Ludwig, Clix, Caseoh, Stableronaldo, Deji (rows 2, 5, 9, 15, 17, 22, 13). Biggest family: 7 of 25.
2. **Solo "explains why" rant over gameplay or stills:** Siege addiction, map ranking, driving, Faker, Elden Ring confession (rows 12, 21, 24, 25, 26, 18, 16). 7 of 25.
3. **Skit or bit with a friend:** hack phone-case, fire-truck bed prank, Fortnite vlog, bed confession (rows 1, 8, 3, 20). 4 of 25.
4. **Reaction to media:** Minecraft bases, Rocket League 2 reveal, the AI image (rows 6, 7, 23). 3 of 25.
5. **Listicle by a third-party editor:** Top 5 HiVise trolling (row 19). 1 of 25.
6. **Recreation of his own old clip** (row 10). 1 of 25. The Stompn 2v5 call (row 4) is also a call clip, so family 1 is 8 by title.

## 4. Hook (first 3 s) (SEEN)
- The hook is a person mid-line or mid-gesture in frame 1 (about 20 of 25). A pinned title over the gameplay appears in 4 of 25 (rows 7, 18, 19, 24), always a "JYNXZI + verb + object" sentence in yellow.
- Clip titles are curiosity hooks that name the other person ("Jynxzi Tells Dantes...", "Jynxzi Hangs Up On Ludwig...").

## 5. Captions and text (SEEN)
- Word-by-word in 23 of 25 (white, black outline, bold condensed sans); yellow accent on the key word in about 15; a few use red for emphasis ("TERRIBLE", "A PRO").
- **Stage directions in asterisks** (*HANGS UP*, *SHOCKED*, *UNPLUGS*, *LAUGHING*, *GETTING HACKED*, *DANTES*): about 8 of 25. New versus Wave 1.
- Placement: on the seam between the two halves, or mid-chest in full-frame clips.
- Pop-in images and memes (the "SONION" onion, a bodybuilder circle, a bathtub-clip thumbnail, a map thumbnail under the chin): about 6 of 25.

## 6. Design (SEEN, counts by eye so approximate)
- **Colour tint on the punchline beat** (red, green, teal, or full grayscale): about 18 of 25. Red = anger or laugh, green = a spike, grayscale = deadpan afterthought (row 4).
- Letterbox gaps filled with a blurred copy of the clip (rows 2, 19).
- INFERENCE: tint flash plus word-by-word captions is the outside "house style".

## 7. Structure (SEEN)
- Setup in the first 2-3 s, one escalation, a payoff line or reaction in the last 20-30%. Almost no clip carries more than one idea.
- The shortest strong clip (12 s, row 26) is one claim and a hand to the mouth; the longest (75 s) is a listicle.

## 8. Audio (FACT, n=25, measured not listened to)
- First 3 s vs typical level: louder by more than 2 dB in 2, quieter by 2 dB or more in 9, about the same in 14; median -0.3 dB (Wave 1: +1.5 dB). No audio hook, same finding as Wave 1.
- Peak seconds (8 dB or more above typical): none in 9 clips, 5 or more in 2 (rows 7 and 19); Wave 1 had 10 clips with 5 or more.
- Loudest second: roughly 6 in the first third, 12 in the middle, 7 in the last third.
- Silence: 24 of 25 clips have 0% near-silent seconds, the worst 3%. Wave 1 had 5 clips over 5%. INFERENCE: outside editors keep audio continuous, consistent with the "dead air hurts" note in Wave 1.

## 9. Comments (SEEN, no usernames)
- They quote the funniest single line ("Jynxzi believes anything", "the survival skills of a fridge", "Sonion"), correct facts (Faker, ranked 3.0, the data cable), or joke at the guest ("now he squats 6000 daily"). Several come from the featured third person.
- INFERENCE: the guest is part of the draw; outside clips are mostly Jynxzi reacting to a guest or a bit.

## 10. Inside (Wave 1) vs outside (Wave 2)
| Thing | Wave 1 (own channel) | Wave 2 (outside) |
|---|---|---|
| Top views | 44.2M | 25.9M |
| Median length | about 34 s | 22 s |
| Clips with 5 or fewer cuts | 15 of 25 | 7 of 25 |
| Captions | word-by-word on the seam, pinned titles | word-by-word, *stage directions*, yellow accent, pop-in images |
| Tint flash on the punchline | some | about 18 of 25 |
| Hook | pinned title or mid-shout | mid-line, or "JYNXZI + verb" pinned title |
| Audio first 3 s vs typical | +1.5 dB | -0.3 dB |

**Reading:** inside clips are longer, one-take, one-idea clips (few cuts, one scream). Outside clips are shorter and busier (more cuts, more graphic punctuation, stage directions) and are usually built around a guest. Both share the same face-top split.

## 11. Limits
- 25 clips, chosen by "first to finish in the queue", not a random sample.
- Counts marked "about" were made by eye on contact sheets and are not exact.
- Audio is measured from the signal; it cannot name a sound effect or music.

# Clipping market analysis (web research, 2026-10-22)

Part 1 of the user's market analysis (pay, platforms, countries, Bangladesh setup). Part 2 (demand per streamer from our database) is at the bottom and is filled in when the database finishes. Tags: **SOURCED** (a page says it, URL given), **VENDOR** (the source sells something in this space, so treat as advertised), **INFERENCE** (my reading), **UNVERIFIED** (could not confirm; check before relying). Nearly all rate data comes from clipping-tool blogs, so every number is "advertised", not audited.

## 1. How the market works
- **Two routes (SOURCED, VENDOR):** (a) campaign marketplaces where a brand or streamer funds a budget, sets a rate per 1,000 verified views, and clippers post clips on their own accounts; (b) direct deals where a streamer recruits clippers (often in a Discord) and pays per view, per clip or a retainer. [clipspeed](https://www.clipspeed.ai/blog/make-money-clipping-streamers.html), [viral.app](https://viral.app/blog/guides/clipping-campaigns)
- **Whop Content Rewards:** the brand sets the rate, a minimum payout that decides when a video enters review, and a maximum payout per video; after approval Whop pays automatically by views. Platforms named: TikTok, YouTube Shorts, X, Instagram Reels. (SOURCED) [Whop docs](https://docs.whop.com/memberships-and-access/third-party-apps/content-rewards)
- **Fees (VENDOR):** one source says Whop charges brands 10% and takes 10% of creators' CPM payouts. [shopiator](https://shopiator.com/blog/whop-content-reward-campaigns)

**Rules that cut the take-home (SOURCED/VENDOR):** per-clip caps ($100-500 typical), first-come budgets (late-verifying views can go unpaid), verification windows, rejections for watermarks or wrong credit tags, minimum views before review, one ban can forfeit pending payouts.

## 3. Rates "in different countries"
- **Campaign pay is set per campaign, not per country (INFERENCE, supported by findclout):** what changes by country is the **audience** the views come from. One brief pays $3 per 1,000 for English content aimed at Tier-1 audiences (US, UK, Canada, Australia) and $1.25 for non-English. Another requires 40% or more of views from Tier-1 countries. FindClout needs at least a 40% US audience on the page and accepts about 1 in 20 applicants. [findclout international](https://findclout.com/blog/clipping-for-international-creators) (SOURCED)
- **Your location mostly does not matter, your audience does (SOURCED, findclout):** clippers in India, the Philippines or almost anywhere work for US brands; verification reads the platform's audience data, not self-reported numbers.
- **If you monetise your own Shorts instead (SOURCED, VENDOR AIR):** YouTube Shorts RPM per 1,000 views by viewer country: US $0.328, Switzerland $0.205, Australia $0.193, South Korea $0.185, UK $0.166, Canada $0.165, Germany $0.163, France $0.102, Brazil $0.045, Mexico $0.040, Philippines $0.023, Indonesia $0.012, India $0.008. Another playbook says $0.01-0.06 typical. Shorts revenue is pooled and the creator gets 45%, so these are rough. Bangladesh: no figure found (UNVERIFIED). [AIR](https://air.io/en/monetization/what-rpm-can-you-expect-from-shorts-in-2026), [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-monetization/)
- **Reading (INFERENCE):** campaign CPM ($1-3) is 3-10 times the Shorts RPM of even the US, so campaigns beat self-monetising for a clipper; the catch is the Tier-1 audience requirement.

## 4. Platform eligibility by country
- **TikTok Creator Rewards (SOURCED, third-party blogs disagree):** most consistent list is US, UK, Germany, France, Brazil, Mexico, Canada; Japan, South Korea, Australia, Italy, Spain in some lists; requirements about 18+, 10,000 followers, 100,000 views in 30 days, videos over one minute. Not in Bangladesh or the Philippines as far as found. Clipping campaigns do not need this program: the campaign pays, not TikTok. [ttcalculator](https://ttcalculator.net/learn/creator-fund-countries/), [timetopost](https://timetopost.co/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-requirements-2026/)
- **YouTube Partner Program:** about 100 countries; Shorts path needs 1,000 subscribers and a views threshold that sources dispute (10M Shorts views in 90 days vs 20M). Bangladesh and India can sign up (a Bangla source and a 2023 Daily Star report); AdSense payment in Bangladesh appears to be cheque or wire only (one blog, UNVERIFIED). [Daily Star](https://online91.thedailystar.net/tech-startup/news/bangladeshi-creators-will-be-able-earn-through-youtube-shorts-3219366)
- **Instagram Reels bonuses:** invite-only and limited to the US, Japan, South Korea; the Reels Play deals ended in the US and India in March 2023 (Meta help page). Not useful as a Bangladesh income source. [Meta](https://help.instagram.com/ipad-app/183392733628561)
- **TikTok access in Bangladesh (UNVERIFIED):** TikTok and YouTube were blocked on 2 August 2024 and later restored per Wikipedia; I found no 2026 status. A Dhaka Tribune feature says Creator Rewards is minimal in Bangladesh and creators rely on sponsors. Check that TikTok and Instagram load on your connection before planning around them.

## 5. Getting paid from Bangladesh (the sensitive part)
- **Whop (SOURCED):** payouts to bank, mobile wallet or crypto wallet in "over 200 countries", but "different countries are eligible for different payout options" (shown in the withdrawal modal). Non-US creators need a bank account in the currency of their registered country; a mismatched currency fails. Standard payout up to 5 business days, instant options exist, some withdrawals go to compliance review. Age 18+. Bangladesh specifically is not named (UNVERIFIED). [Whop payouts](https://docs.whop.com/manage-your-business/manage-payouts/payout-methods)
- **PayPal (SOURCED, findclout):** Bangladesh, Pakistan and Nigeria are not listed as PayPal Payouts countries. Bangladesh Bank has been clearing the way for PayPal and Payoneer (reports July 2026) but PayPal is not clearly live. [Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/amp/business/banks/416263/bb-paves-way-for-paypal-international-payment)
- **Payoneer (VENDOR, nsave):** the common route for Bangladeshi freelancers; fees about 1% receiving, up to 3.5% FX spread, $29.95 annual fee below an activity threshold; a PayPal integration added late 2025 (UNVERIFIED). **Wise:** residents cannot open a multi-currency account or receive USD details (VENDOR). [nsave](https://www.nsave.com/bangladesh/paypal)
- **US ACH-style personal USD account (VENDOR, nsave):** same type of US account details Upwork and Fiverr use for payouts; direct SWIFT bank transfer is the other option. Vyro payout rails: Stripe or PayPal per one review, others say bank or crypto (UNVERIFIED; Stripe availability for Bangladesh was not found).
- **Bangladesh Bank, freelancer circular (SOURCED, July 2026):** freelancers can prove foreign income with electronic evidence (platform statements, emails); inward remittance up to $20,000 without formal declaration; payments through online gateways up to $10,000 per transaction; ICT freelancers may keep up to 50% of export earnings in foreign currency (30% for other service exporters). Read the circular itself before relying on the numbers. [Daily Star](https://tds-images.thedailystar.net/business/news/bangladesh-bank-eases-forex-rules-freelancers-4229851), [Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/amp/business/banks/415725/bb-eases-foreign-transactions-policy-for)
- **Crypto/USDT (SOURCED, grey):** Bangladesh Bank still opposes crypto and treats it as prohibited under the Foreign Exchange Regulation Act 1947 and the Money Laundering Prevention Act 2012; use is widespread (Chainalysis rank 13 of 151, 2025) and enforcement against individuals is not clearly documented. Legal exposure is real; get local advice before using it. [Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/amp/bangladesh/dhaka/402188/young-bangladeshis-turn-to-cryptocurrency-despite)
- **Tax (SOURCED, findclout):** US platforms commonly ask non-US creators for IRS Form W-8BEN; local tax rules apply; keep a record of every payout and conversion. Currency conversion costs on every payout (PayPal lists a 3% markup in its US fee page); test one real payout first.

## 6. VPN: what is sensible
- **Verification reads audience data from the platform (SOURCED):** Whop checks views against the actual post; findclout checks the audience via connected accounts. A VPN does not change where viewers live, and using one to fake an audience location or inflate views is read as fraud (a consumer source says TikTok may act against VPN-inflated metrics; UNVERIFIED policy text). Practical result (INFERENCE): a VPN will not unlock Tier-1 pay, and using it to meet a geo condition risks a ban and forfeited payouts.
- **When a VPN is reasonable (INFERENCE):** reaching a platform that is blocked on your connection, or privacy; keep each account on one consistent identity and real details, and ask each campaign's chat how it treats location.
- **Better lever for Tier-1 pay:** make English content that the US/UK audience actually watches; the audience requirement is about who watches, and the account's own Insights shows follower countries once it has 100 followers. Do not buy views or run engagement pods (flagged, lowers the US share).

## 7. Setup checklist (what is required to start)
1. **Identity:** age 18+ (Whop withdrawals, Vyro payout verification), a real government ID for KYC at first withdrawal, a payout method that works from Bangladesh (see section 5) tested with one small real payout.
2. **Accounts:** dedicated TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram accounts per campaign niche (not your personal one); connect them so views can be tracked; check each loads in Bangladesh.
3. **Source material rights:** only clip from the source a campaign authorises (their VOD or content folder); posting someone's stream without a campaign, deal or published permission risks copyright strikes and account termination; music is the usual trigger (SOURCED, [clipspeed](https://www.clipspeed.ai/blog/can-you-clip-someone-elses-stream.html)).
4. **Format:** 9:16, 1080x1920, about 15-45 s, required credit tags, no third-party watermarks, vary captions and first frames so identical reposts are not flagged.
5. **Campaign hygiene:** read each brief (country list, 40% Tier-1 rule, caps, banned content); stop submitting when the remaining budget falls under about 10%.
6. **Scam filters (SOURCED, findclout):** no activation or registration fees, no password sharing, no fake payout-release emails, no paid view boosting, be wary of unsolicited recruiters.
7. **Money plan:** a payout rail with the lowest all-in cost, a W-8BEN if asked, and a record of income for Bangladeshi tax.

## 8. Reading for our project (INFERENCE)
- The money is in **campaign CPM from streamers and brands** ($1-3 typical, higher for gambling or crypto niches) with a **Tier-1 audience**, not platform payouts.
- A gaming/streamer niche pays at the low end per view; volume and clip quality decide income. Our format study (Waves 1-2) is what makes clips that earn: short, one idea, a face-top split, word-by-word captions.
- Open decisions for the user: which payout rail from Bangladesh, whether crypto is acceptable, and which streamer to clip first (Part 2 below).

## Addendum A (2026-10-22, second research round)
### Bangladesh payout routes, more detail
- **Whop to bKash/Nagad (UNVERIFIED):** no source confirms that Whop pays to bKash, Nagad or Bangladeshi banks. Third-party payout APIs list bKash, Nagad and bank routing as Bangladesh account types (bKash minimum 50 BDT, maximum 250,000 BDT in one provider) but that describes those providers, not Whop. Check Whop's withdrawal modal. [dLocal](https://docs.dlocal.com/docs/bangladesh-payouts)
- **Payoneer (VENDOR, elevatepay/nsave):** withdrawal to a Bangladeshi bank, to bKash (minimum 1,000 BDT in one guide) or at an ATM with the card; no taka receiving account, so clients pay in another currency first; withdrawal fee band 1.2% to 4% (the flat $1.50 fee excludes Bangladesh and Pakistan); an annual account fee applies if you receive under about $6,000 in 12 months; the exchange-rate markup can cost more than the visible fees (about 1.5% on a $900 withdrawal in one estimate); the PayPal-funded option is not open to Bangladesh recipients. Confirm every figure on your own Payoneer screen. [elevatepay](https://www.elevatepay.co/blog/is-paypal-available-in-bangladesh), [nsave](https://www.nsave.com/bangladesh/payoneer)
- **Tax (SOURCED, conflicting, June 2026):** the Observer reported that banks were deducting 7.5% withholding tax on foreign-client income; the NBR said there is no such provision and the prime minister reportedly said it would not apply to freelancers or content creators; people still report being charged. Not settled; ask a Bangladeshi chartered accountant and check the NBR website before choosing a rail. Personal income tax-free threshold BDT 375,000 in one secondary guide. [Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/business/411758/nbr-no-tax-deduction-from-freelancers-income), [Observer](https://observerbd.com/news/578567)

### More platforms (VENDOR unless stated)
- **Clipping.net:** per-campaign payout method (PayPal, Cash App, Zelle or crypto), account verified by a code in your bio, per-campaign minimum view thresholds (clips under the cutoff earn nothing), payouts when a cycle closes; one review (ClipAffiliates, a competitor, July 2026) calls it legit with caveats. [review](https://www.clipaffiliates.com/blog/clipping-net-review)
- **ClipFarm (Airrack):** runs on Whop Content Rewards, weekly payouts per its own page. [ClipFarm](https://contentrewards.com/clipfarm)
- **Whop fraud checks:** each video gets a 0-100 bot score; the company says it paid more than $1.7M to over 98,000 creators (company figure). [opus.pro](https://www.opus.pro/blog/whop-content-rewards)
- **Payout delays (VENDOR, OpenClip):** three gates between a view and money: view verification, a minimum payout threshold, and the platform's payment schedule. Balances spread across many campaigns can stay below the minimum indefinitely; name mismatches and unsupported countries show up only at withdrawal. [OpenClip payouts and tax](https://openclip.app/clipping/clipping-payouts-and-tax)
- **Where clippers are (SOURCED, one article):** an iGaming industry piece says top earners on one operation are in India, the Philippines and Serbia, so clipping from South Asia is normal; no source found on Bangladesh-specific rejections (UNVERIFIED). [igamingexpert](https://igamingexpert.com/news/affiliates/clipping-threat-black-market-advertising/?amp=1)
- **Individual reviews are mixed (anecdote):** a Trustpilot reviewer of one platform says earnings "evaporated", another says paid on time. Start with small tests and withdraw early.

### Practical order for a Bangladesh start (INFERENCE)
1. Confirm which payout options Whop and Vyro show for Bangladesh in your own dashboard before creating accounts.
2. Open a Payoneer account (or the local bank route that the July 2026 circular supports) and test one small withdrawal to see the real all-in cost.
3. Ask a Bangladeshi accountant about the 7.5% deduction question.
4. Start with one campaign, one account, English content aimed at a US/UK audience.

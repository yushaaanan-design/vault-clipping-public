# Format trends (compare over time)

Method: at each snapshot, take the current top 100 shorts of the studied channels, tag each with a format ID from [[Format Catalog]], and record the share of the top 100 and the median views per format. Compare snapshots to see which formats rise or fade. Take a snapshot at least monthly and after any big shift. Views are rounded YouTube figures; do not compare raw views across very different ages without noting upload dates.

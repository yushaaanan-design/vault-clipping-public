## F01 Split-screen reaction (face top, gameplay bottom, pinned caption)
- Build: vertical frame; top half the streamer's face very close; bottom half the game; one caption pinned the whole time. Screen flashes with game state (red or green). 2 cuts in 10 s.
- Hook: no setup, the first second is already the key line ("Green light!"). Peak scream near 80%; payoff line at the end.
- Replay curve: front-loaded (0.79 in the first tenth, 0.07 in the last).
- Source: [[Viral Library/viral-patterns]].

## F02 Zoomed-face trivia explainer
- Build: streamer's face fills the frame; for the first 6 s a picture of the subject (Faker) on the lower half; one large white word with black outline in the middle, one word per beat; a real photo pops up when the key noun is said (Faker Tower).
- Hook: a surprising claim as the first sentence; ends on a credibility line.
- No dead air: all 20 s voiced, 2 cuts.

## F03 Countdown Top-5 compilation
- Build: pinned headline, numbered list that fills as each entry plays, source clip in the middle over a blurred copy of itself, original view-count watermark as proof on the #1 entry.
- Same format, 4.7M vs 93k vs 32k views, so the format alone is not the driver. Visible differences in the weak ones: 100 s long and slowed, small video window with big black bars, in-joke labels that mean nothing to newcomers.

## F04 Streamer drama
- Build: text headline in second 0 promising "the real reason"; two facecams stacked on top; word-by-word captions; the actual argument ("you have 100 times the ego that I have") lands at 34-54 s.
- Community angle: the audience already knows both people.

## How to add a format
Copy a section, assign the next ID (F08...), link 3+ example clip notes in Viral Library/Clips/, and write the date first seen. Never rename an ID (trends depend on it).

Public streamer, mostly Rainbow Six Siege, with a large clip ecosystem (his own Shorts channel plus many third-party clip channels). Everything here comes from 6 clips the user supplied, 401 channel titles and the user's own remarks. TODO sections get filled from the 400-clip study and the comment data.

## Update 2026-10-22: what the top 25 clips on his channel show (read frame by frame; details in [[Viral Library/Wave 1 Top 25 Own Channel]])
- **Three content families**: facecam over gameplay (14 of 25, mostly "he reacts to a clip" and his own clutches), IRL or collab talk with no gameplay (9: CaseOh banter, fan gifts, a viewer's grandma, a setup tour, sports), and cheap TikTok re-uploads (2).
- **One idea per clip**, tension and a payoff; many top clips have 2-5 cuts; length ranges 10-60 s with no link to views (44M at 10 s, 22M at 58 s).
- **The premise is stated in text in frame 1**: a pinned label or title (neon-blue comic font for superlatives like "200 IQ CLUTCH", "BEST STRAT OF THE YEAR"; white rounded label for the premise or a teammate's quote).
- **Captions**: one word at a time, white comic italic with black outline on the seam or mid-frame; colour-coded per speaker when two people talk.
- **Collab pull**: CaseOh and Sketch in 3 of the top 25; a guest like Tyler1 in another; the community loves his reactions to better players, fan gifts and unexpected politeness.
- **Comments quote a single line or moment** ("So let me get this-", "Amaru didn't work", "Welcome to the rank squad", "most respectful man for a solid minute"). A clipper should look for moments that produce a quotable line.
- **Titles are curiosity hooks** and often do not describe the clip; the on-screen label explains it.

# Clipping-specific preferences (the global ones are in the central harness Preferences)

- **No per-clip write-ups.** Analysis of each clip is for my own cross-checking; outputs are aggregate: formats, patterns, playbook, comment themes (user, 2026-10-22).
- **Streamers are personal.** Communities have a personal relationship with their streamer; learn running jokes, rivalries and collabs, not only clip text (user, 2026-10-22).
- **Hits AND flops.** Study why clips flopped, inside and outside the streamer's own channel (user, 2026-10-22).
- **Deletion.** Downloaded videos are deleted after analysis; keep metadata, comment text without usernames, contact sheets (user, 2026-10-22).
- **First automatic test was rejected** ("no virality whatsoever"): do not automate clip picking again before the format and virality findings are in.
- **The user will send reels directly** (YouTube, Instagram, other) and wants Claude to analyse why they work; the Telegram content-analysis bot comes later and is separate from the Instagram reel bot.

- **Context from folder, never chat (user, 2026-10-23).** Every analysis goes to disk (clips.db reads table, Format Library note, run-log) as it happens; the chat is disposable. Measure the hook (first word time, cuts in first 3 s, first-3 s loudness, question/number in the first words) and everything else, and keep style, hook, positioning and format for every clip so later analysis can learn what performs and what does not.
